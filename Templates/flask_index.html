Fake News

In this modern world, People across the globe are connected to each other via news.Nowdays with the access of Internet and Networking, People can get the updates of any city,state,country,continent and so on.Now with the occurency of any event in a particular time and location,Through news we can get instant updates.But the real challenge we face today is to determine whether the received news is true or not. We all are familiar with the phase Fake News Travels Faster. With the rapid spread of fake news in society,It can lead to severe consequences.It results in Misinformed Publicwhich lead them to make decisions based on false information, affecting everything from health to politics.Fake News can cause Political Manipulation which can sway elections, incite political unrest, and damage democratic institutions. There are Economic Consequences which caused by false financial news which can cause stock market fluctuations and harm businesses.The great severe cansequences can seen in Health Risks which spreads Misinformation about diseases, vaccines, and treatments can lead to harmful choices. There are also cases where fake news results in Social Unrest & Violence. Fake news can incite riots, communal violence, and mob lynchings. Apart from them,Fake News can also cause Legal & Ethical Issueswhich results the Individuals and organizations to face defamation lawsuits or legal consequences for spreading false information.But excluding all of these,There is one thing which is harmed to the great extent. That is the Trust of public in media,government and institutions.
